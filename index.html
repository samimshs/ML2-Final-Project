About

Engineering clarity from raw data.

KabulHub is a research, data, and technology company based in Chicago. Our work spans data science, economics, machine learning, software engineering, education, and applied research.

We care less about shipping features and more about whether the result actually helps someone decide, learn, or build.

Core Values

  1. Truth over assumptionWe seek evidence before conclusions.
  2. Clarity through simplicityComplex problems deserve clear, elegant systems.
  3. Impact over activityThe value of work is measured by better outcomes.
